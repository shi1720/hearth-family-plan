# Launch evidence and remaining operating requirements

## Implemented and exercised

- Persistent private planning records, explicit evidence states, permissions and invitation/revocation routes.
- Owner-scoped Meta credential encryption and file encryption; no key in exports or source.
- Consent-based native PDF/image transcription, human acceptance, quoted source proposals and cross-record review.
- Exact arithmetic, reserves, claim/estate authority separation, evidence gates and source-linked task instructions.
- Real tests and type/build checks documented in tests and README; no tests establish professional qualification or legal validity.

## Before accepting real legal/administrative service responsibility

- Engage qualified Florida counsel and an estate-operations owner. Check permitted administrative/insurance assistance, professional independence, scope, conflicts, fee/referral arrangements and insurance.
- Define professional identity/qualification checks. Current invitations establish application membership only; they are not credential verification.
- Obtain independent security testing and resolve findings. Test hosted access policies, key rotation, backups/restore, monitoring, incident response, retention/deletion, data-access jurisdictions and service continuity.
- Execute appropriate client/privacy/processor agreements. Account ownership, legal representatives, named insurance beneficiaries and sponsors require distinct permissions and engagements.
- Establish institution-specific current forms and authorised submission processes. This app prepares review packets and records supplied receipts; it does not file legal or insurer forms or certify that a provider has accepted a submission.
- Establish paper or qualified external execution/custody routes. Hearth does not become an electronic-will custodian or verify that no later will exists.
- Validate paid service economics and staffing. The Practice page is a proposed business case, not a staffed care team, contract or current service promise.

## Deliberate external boundaries

No autonomous money movement, medical/mental-health advice, tax planning, contested estate resolution, universal global wills, insurer coverage determinations, charity effectiveness rankings or model-based capacity judgments. A recorded receipt is a user's evidence record; independent bank/insurer verification requires a separately implemented authorised connection.

Originals remain important. Keep independent exports and physical/legal originals where required. Hashes can support version checks but do not establish identity, consent, witnessing, validity, title, absence of a later will, or immunity to challenge.

## Demo versus real records

The built-in sample is fictional and labelled throughout. Owner self-attestation of authority is allowed only in that sample for demonstrating state transitions. Real household authority review requires an invited administration-scoped professional member. This remains an attestation, not independent court/registry verification. No real estate, account or charity payment is fabricated by the demo.
