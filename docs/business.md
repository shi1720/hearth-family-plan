## Customer and purchase

The buyer is the owner/COO or head of client service at an independent financial-advice firm that already coordinates estate preparation and surviving-family work. Qualify firms on actual unfinished arrangements, repeated chasing, an accountable service lead, legal-delivery partners, client consent and a funded budget. ## Illustrative economics

At 250 households, 15 annual operations minutes each at $40/hour costs $2,500. Add 12.5 professional-oversight hours at $160 ($2,000), infrastructure/support $1,500 and account management $1,500. Direct cost is $7,500; contribution before acquisition and company overhead is $10,500 (58.3%). Oversight is not individual legal representation.

If annual operations take one hour per household, cost rises $7,500 and contribution falls to $3,000 (16.7%). Unlimited care and unresponsive-household chasing cannot fit the base promise. At $60 per avoidable internal hour, an $18,000 fee needs 300 hours of annual savings if labour alone justifies it. Do not count saved internal time and paid service work twice.

A $3,000 administration case with 20 operator hours at $40, one oversight hour at $160 and $150 other cost contributes $1,890 (63%) before overhead. At 60 operator hours, only $290 remains. Legal/tax representation, court fees and complex property work remain separate.

Inference is metered, not assumed free. Standard-mode Muse Spark and Wonder incur token charges; file processing and lengthy reasoning can change cost. Hearth caps hourly calls and output sizes and records model usage alongside saved inputs. Measure inference, all human time, storage, security and support before setting commercial quotas. The seeded firm/value figures are illustrative, not a live portfolio.

## Market entry and competition

First validate with advice firms and their existing estate-law/operations partners. Ask for a priced, limited pilot and compare the entire household journey against the current software plus people. Do not infer demand from total adviser counts or treat client relationships as unique households.

Empathy already combines planning and human support; Wealth.com and Vanilla serve advisers; Trust & Will and other providers supply inexpensive documents; Elayne and Alix perform estate administration. The business is not defensible because it stores documents or uses AI. The potential advantage is a trusted distribution relationship, institution-specific completion procedures, dependable professional delivery, portable records and lower total work.

## Four validation gates

1. Three qualified firms describe actual recurring work and share a lawful representative sample.
2. Two commit to a defined paid pilot with separately engaged legal/operations professionals.
3. Measure household effort, total staff time, completion evidence, errors and accepted institution outcomes against the existing process.
4. Prove annual readiness is viable without relying on unpredictable estate revenue; demonstrate a scope and cost model that remains sustainable when exceptions rise.

Do not contact prospects or claim partnerships on the strength of this planning document. A later legal/operations engagement is a separate external action requiring real participants.
