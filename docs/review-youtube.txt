YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION
A folder full of documents is only the beginning.

Hearth helps a family locate important originals, compare recorded account instructions with current wishes, choose what helpers can see, and track the evidence behind the next step. Meta Muse Spark reads a consented sample document. Muse Wonder reviews connected records and proposes source-backed follow-ups for a person to accept or reject.

This demonstration uses the fictional Maria Ellis family. The people, accounts, institutions and external confirmations shown are illustrative. Hearth records permitted work; it does not execute wills, certify authority, file claims, make donations or move money. The initial planning guidance is scoped to Florida and links to primary sources.

The proposed customer is an independent advice firm. The pricing and staff-time savings shown are business hypotheses, not validated revenue or customer results.

ProjectDescription
Having a will is not the same as having a plan the family can use. The original may be hard to find. An old account statement may conflict with the person's current wishes. A trusted helper may not know which records they may access.

Hearth connects those practical handoffs in one private workspace. Families record people and responsibilities, documents and original locations, account and life-policy instructions, optional giving and evidence-backed next steps. Meta reads a consented PDF or image; the owner checks the transcription before analysis. Cross-record review proposes practical questions with exact source quotes. A person accepts or rejects each suggestion. Accepting one creates a task, not a legally binding instruction.

Permission-aware sharing keeps care information findable without exposing every private account or wish. Death reporting and recorded authority review remain separate. Insurance records distinguish preparation, submission, approval and actual payment. An exportable preparation packet organises the next authorised step. The estate ledger separates proposed and confirmed entries and holds a recorded reserve. Giving intentions remain separate from implemented instructions and receipts.

Our initial buyer is an independent financial-advice firm with continuing household coordination work. The proposed $18,000 annual readiness service covers 250 households with bounded work and separately scoped professional services. At an assumed $60 per avoidable staff hour, that fee needs 300 genuine annual hours saved if labour alone justifies purchase. No customer, paid pilot or measured saving is claimed yet.

Shivam Gupta directed and built the original application with substantial AI assistance during the event. Framework dependencies, infrastructure and source references are credited separately. The demonstration uses a clearly fictional family and original synthetic documents. Hearth does not provide legal representation, certify execution or authority, submit court or insurer forms, make donations, or move money. Appropriate people and institutions perform those external actions.

describeMeta use
Hearth uses Meta Model API directly. Standard-mode Muse Spark 1.3 reads explicitly consented PDF/image attachments and prepares structured source-text intake proposals. The owner compares the transcription with the original before accepting it. Standard-mode Muse Wonder 1.0 reviews the connected family plan for practical gaps and inconsistencies across documents, people, account instructions and open tasks.

Server-side response validation requires structured fields and rejects quoted evidence that does not occur exactly in the submitted source. Proposals remain pending until a person accepts or dismisses them. Accepted tasks preserve their explanation and quote. The application, rather than the model, enforces access and state transitions. Model timeouts and usage caps preserve existing records and do not fabricate replacement results.

whyMeta important
The useful intelligence is connecting different records, rather than answering a generic question about wills. Multimodal reading lets the family begin with a document it already has. Cross-record reasoning can surface the practical difference between a reported wish, an institution's recorded instruction and an unresolved confirmation. Exact source excerpts make a proposed next step inspectable. Hearth combines those model capabilities with deterministic permissions, evidence states, version history and bookkeeping so assistance can become reviewable work without turning an AI suggestion into legal authority.

partnerfeedback plus opinions
We used Meta Model API directly; we are not claiming production use of Baseten, Modal or Fireworks in Hearth. We have no direct experience-based feedback on those partner inference services from this build.

Our feedback on Meta tooling is that multimodal input and structured output make evidence-oriented workflows possible, while careful product design still needs to handle transcription mistakes, incomplete structured responses, latency and uncertain interpretations. Exact-quote validation and human confirmation were important to this application. We would value clearer model-specific examples for document input, consistent structured-output behavior, and detailed usage visibility for multimodal calls. These are observations and requests from this build, not benchmark comparisons or claims about every model request.
